# ADR-0001: Demand-first, capability-independent discovery
Status: Accepted | Date: 2026-11-15

## Context
A capability-first marketplace only surfaces work the platform already knows how to perform. That excludes valuable unmet needs and prevents the OS from creating new products or capabilities.

## Decision
Discover and validate demand independently of current capability. Only after an opportunity and solution are assessed does Capability Foundry determine reuse, composition, build, acquisition, integration, human contribution, partnership, deferral, or rejection.

## Consequences
- Market discovery cannot filter by current agent/tool inventory.
- Capability gaps become explicit first-class records.
- Build/acquire/partner decisions require cost, risk, approval, and readiness evidence.
- Not every discovered signal becomes an executable mission.
