# DAYANEXA Command Center

One command center presents a coherent view of the OS while preserving domain-specific workflows.

## Workspaces
1. **Executive Command:** North Star, verified outcomes, cash/revenue, margin, risk, bottlenecks.
2. **Market Radar:** source health, new signals, trends, connector permissions.
3. **Demand Intelligence:** hypotheses, evidence, clusters, requester validation, review queue.
4. **Opportunity Universe:** opportunity portfolio, prioritization, confidence, risk, stage.
5. **Solution Studio:** problem framing, alternatives, solution design, customer value.
6. **Capability Foundry:** inventory, gaps, build/buy/partner plans, readiness.
7. **Product & Service Catalog:** products, services, packages, versions, pricing.
8. **Agent Workforce:** agents, tools, permissions, evaluation, health, cost.
9. **Execution Control:** missions, task graphs, assignments, run state, exceptions.
10. **Commercial Control:** customers, offers, quotes, contracts, approvals.
11. **Quality & Delivery:** artifacts, review, acceptance, revision, dispute.
12. **Economic Ledger:** revenue, costs, invoices, payment verification, payouts, reconciliation.
13. **Intelligence & Learning:** trends, outcome analysis, reusable patterns.
14. **Governance & Audit:** policies, approvals, permissions, audit events, incidents.

## UX principles
- Every important number links to its underlying records and evidence.
- Distinguish observed, inferred, estimated, approved, and verified data visually.
- Show why an opportunity was prioritized and what evidence is missing.
- Surface blocked workflows and the precise approval/action needed.
- Never imply an agent is running when it is queued, paused, failed, or awaiting authorization.
- Use progressive disclosure; keep the executive view legible and operational workspaces detailed.
