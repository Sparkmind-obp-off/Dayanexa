# Product Roadmap

Roadmap is gated by evidence and operational readiness, not calendar promises.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- Product constitution, domain model, architecture, threat model.
- Repository scaffold, coding standards, CI checks, environment boundaries.
- Authentication and role model design.
**Exit:** docs approved, app builds, no secrets in repo, architecture decisions recorded.

## Phase 1 — Market Intelligence
- Source registry and authorized connector framework.
- Signal capture, normalization, provenance, deduplication.
- Demand hypothesis and evidence review UI.
**Exit:** traceable signals and auditable evidence; no unapproved source access.

## Phase 2 — Opportunity & Solution Intelligence
- Demand clusters, multidimensional assessments, opportunity portfolio.
- Solution design and customer validation records.
**Exit:** opportunity decisions explainable and evidence-backed.

## Phase 3 — Capability Foundry
- Capability registry, gap analysis, build/buy/partner decision records.
- Readiness tests and cost estimates.
**Exit:** capability cannot be marked available without test evidence.

## Phase 4 — Agentic Execution
- Durable orchestrator, task graph, agent/tool registry, sandbox.
- Human review, retries, idempotency, QA.
**Exit:** controlled pilot completes bounded work safely.

## Phase 5 — Commercialization & Economics
- Offer, quote, approval, mission, acceptance, invoice/payment reconciliation.
- Cost and margin ledger; contributor settlement controls.
**Exit:** verified economic event and reconciled cost/revenue in a controlled pilot.

## Phase 6 — Learning & Expansion
- Repeat-demand detection, reusable products, capability reuse, performance analytics.
- More segments and connectors after policy review.
**Exit:** repeatable unit economics and operational reliability.

## Explicitly out of initial scope
Unbounded autonomous web scraping, public auto-posting, automatic contract acceptance, unapproved paid APIs, automatic money movement, open freelancer marketplace, and high-risk/high-stakes execution.
